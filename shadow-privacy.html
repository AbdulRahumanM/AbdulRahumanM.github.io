Privacy note — a conversation about how dietitians work

This research is run by me, Abdul, personally, in Sydney. There is no company behind this page or this research. I intend to handle personal information consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What I collect
If you book a time or email me, I will have your name and contact details, and ordinary booking or email metadata. In a conversation, and in any later shadowing, I will only note what you choose to share about your work and how the work gets done — not information that identifies a patient. I will record and transcribe a session only if you opt in.
How I use it
I use this information only for this research: to understand how Australian dietitians work, to write a findings summary, and to send you that summary if you would like a copy. I will not sell your information, and I will not use it to pitch a product.
Storage
I store notes, transcripts and, if you opt in, recordings on my own password-protected computer, which only I use. Booking details stay in Cal.com and emails stay in my email account.
Retention
Within 30 days of our conversation I delete any recording and remove your name, practice and other identifying details from the transcript and notes. I keep those de-identified transcripts and notes for up to 2 years, then delete them. You can ask me to delete anything sooner.
Recording
I record and transcribe a session only if you opt in. You can say no. We can work from notes alone.
Patient information
I will not collect patient-identifiable information. I am interested in workflow and process, not the content of patient records or a patient’s story.
Shadowing
Shadowing proceeds only with permission from the practice or workplace, and with patient consent where a patient’s care could be observed.
Access, correction and deletion
If you would like to know what I hold, have it corrected, or have it deleted, email me at abdulrahumanfazi@gmail.com and I will deal with that.
Website visits
This site counts page visits with GoatCounter, which does not use cookies and does not identify you. It records things like which page was viewed and which site linked to it.
Publishing
I will not publish your name or practice-identifying details without your agreement. If it helps your practice, I am happy to sign a confidentiality or research agreement before we meet.
Contact
Privacy questions: abdulrahumanfazi@gmail.com
