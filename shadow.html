What it involves

Pick whatever format you're comfortable with.

In person

If you're in the Atlanta metro area, I'll come to your office and observe on-site — one visit, nothing recurring.

Remote

Elsewhere, this works just as well as a screen-share call — you go about a normal working session, undisturbed, while I quietly observe.

Q&A only

Not ready to have someone watch? We can just talk — a guided conversation about your workflow instead, no observation at all.

Time

30–60 minutes, scheduled around your day.

I'm trying to understand

How your day-to-day workflow runs — intake, scheduling, documentation, patient communication, follow-up — not one narrow slice of it.

I'm not doing

Disrupting your workflow, recording patient data, or pitching a product for you to buy.

Why I'm doing this

A hypothesis I don't want to guess my way into.

Independent primary care practices run on workflows that were never really designed — they accumulated: intake, scheduling, chart documentation, coordinating with other providers, following up with patients, all layered on top of each other over years. I'm exploring an early concept, Project Aster, for software that could take some of that manual load off physicians and staff.

That's a hypothesis, not a finished product — I've spent my career watching well-meaning tools get built for clinicians without ever sitting with them first. Before I build anything, I want to see the actual day-to-day workflow, to understand where time and effort are really going, and where a tool would help versus just get in the way.

Privacy, PHI & confidentiality

The direct answer, upfront.

  • I observe workflow and process — how work moves through your day and gets done — not the content of specific patient records.
  • No PHI is recorded, photographed, or written down. If a chart or message happens to be visible on screen, it's incidental to observing the process, never the point of the exercise.
  • Nothing from the session is shared outside this research — no names, no practice-identifying details, without your explicit sign-off.
  • There's no product yet, no data pipeline, no integration — this is me in the room (or on a call) with a notebook, not software touching your system.
  • Happy to sign an NDA, and if it helps your practice's compliance process, we can put a simple confidentiality/research agreement in writing before we meet — just ask.
