A conversation about how dietitians work

I’d like to learn how your work as a dietitian actually runs.

Hi, I’m Abdul, a product leader now living in Sydney. For seven years at Twin Health in the US, I built AI tools, CGM and wearable experiences, and the app members used every day, side by side with the dietitians and clinicians on our care teams.

Together we changed how those dietitians went about their day. Our work gave them back 300+ hours a month, so each could support more people with truly individualised care. Now I want to understand how dietitians here work. This research is my own.

You’re the expert in your own work; I’m here to learn, not to pitch. We can meet at your practice in Sydney, over video from anywhere in Australia, or in a quiet gap in the day.

Book a conversation of about 30–45 minutes Prefer a 15-minute intro call first?

Who it’s for

Practice owners and employed dietitians, including new graduates, in private practice, hospital, aged care and NDIS, anywhere in Australia. Sydney and NSW first.

What’s involved

A 30–45 minute conversation, around your day: in person in Sydney, or by video from anywhere in Australia. Optional 2–4 hour shadowing, only with practice or workplace permission and patient consent if a patient could be observed. Saying no is fine.

What you get

You get an early copy of the findings summary, so you can see how your peers work. Your experience shapes whether anything gets built for dietitians, and what. Nothing to buy.

Why are you doing this?

I saw what changed for dietitians in the US when the tools fitted their day. Before deciding whether anything like that is worth building in Australia, I want to understand how the work actually runs here. Sometimes the right answer is to build nothing. Your experience is what that decision should rest on.

Privacy

Australian Privacy Principles apply. Recording only if you opt in. No patient-identifiable information. Read the full privacy note.

Questions

Who are you?

I’m a product leader who spent seven years at Twin Health in the US, building AI tools and CGM and wearable experiences side by side with dietitians and clinicians. I now live in Sydney. This research is my own, and I’m not selling anything. More about me: abdulrahuman.com (opens in a new tab) and LinkedIn (opens in a new tab).

Do I have to agree to shadowing?

No. The conversation is the main invitation. Shadowing is optional, later, and only if your workplace can approve it.

Will this be recorded?

Only if you opt in. Saying no is fine. We can have a useful conversation from notes alone.

I’m a new graduate. Does that count?

Yes. New graduates (under 2 years practising as an APD) are in scope, as are experienced APDs.

I work outside Sydney or NSW.

You’re welcome. We can talk by video from anywhere in Australia.

What about patients if you shadow?

Shadowing only happens with practice or workplace permission, and with patient consent where a patient’s care could be observed. I will not collect patient-identifiable information.

What happens to my notes?

Identifying details are removed within 30 days, and de-identified notes are deleted after 2 years. Read the full privacy note.
